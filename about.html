About Emagon

Fifteen years of protecting Abuja's sight

Emagon Eye Clinic opened its doors in Maitama in 2011 with a single consulting room and one mission: to bring internationally-trained eye care to Nigeria's capital, without the wait times or travel of seeking treatment abroad.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class, affordable eye care to every patient who walks through our doors — from routine checks to complex surgery.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where preventable blindness is rare, because quality eye care is close, fast and trusted.

Our Values

Clinical excellence, honest communication, and treating every patient like family — not a file number.

Our Facility

Purpose-built for modern eye care

Digital diagnostics suite

OCT scanners, visual field analysers and digital fundus imaging.

Laser correction theatre

A dedicated LASIK suite with same-day recovery rooms.

In-house optical shop

Frames, sunglasses and contact lenses dispensed on-site.

Children's eye unit

A friendly, playful space designed for young patients.
